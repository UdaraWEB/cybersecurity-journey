TryHackMe - Water Bottle (Write-up )
Overview
I recently solved the "Water Bottle" OSINT challenge on TryHackMe. This challenge is all about finding a specific location in Mandaluyong, Philippines, and tracking down a water refilling station that used to be there in the past.
What I Learned
The coolest part of this challenge was learning how to use Google Maps Street View more effectively. I learned:
Time Travel with Maps: How to use the "See more dates" feature to look at historical imagery.
Attention to Detail: Looking at shop signs and phone numbers from years ago to find specific info.
Connecting Dots: Using a partial phone number to verify the right business.
How I Solved It
Finding the Street: I started by searching for Boni Avenue in Mandaluyong on Google Maps.
Going Back in Time: The current view didn't show the station, so I switched the Street View date to 2014.
Spotting the Target: While moving down the street, I found a shop called "Aquabest".
Getting the Info: I looked closely at their sign and found the phone number starting with 63922.
The Flag: Combining the name and the number gave me the final answer.
Flag: THM{aquabest_639228721288}
Conclusion
This was a fun, quick challenge that reminded me how much data is hidden in plain sight if you just know how to look at the past!
